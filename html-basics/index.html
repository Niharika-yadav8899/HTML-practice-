A paragraph is a self-contained section of written text—made up of one or more sentences—that focuses on a single idea, point, or speaker. In writing and typography, it typically starts on a new line and is marked by an indentation or a blank line break. Key Parts of a Paragraph • Topic sentence: States the main idea or focus of the paragraph (often the first sentence). • Supporting sentences: Provide facts, examples, explanations, or details that develop the main idea. • Concluding or transition sentence: Wraps up the point or links smoothly to the next paragraph.
